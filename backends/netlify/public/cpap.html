Import a CPAP SD card

Reads a ResMed AirSense 11 card in this browser and stores each Night's AHI, usage, leak and pressure, every event, and a minute-by-minute leak trace. Only the summary, settings, event and trend files are opened, and of the trend files only the leak signal is decoded; the breathing waveforms are never opened, and nothing is sent until you press Upload.

Card

The machine keeps its own clock. Its offset from UTC is read from the card when you pick it; until then, or if the card has none, this computer's time zone is used. Type an offset such as -05:00, or a time zone such as America/Toronto, to read the card another way.

Pick the card's top folder, the one holding STR.edf and DATALOG.

Import a CPAP SD card

Reads a ResMed AirSense 11 card in this browser and stores each Night's AHI, usage, leak and pressure, every event, and a minute-by-minute leak trace. Only the summary, event and trend files are opened, and of the trend files only the leak signal is decoded; the breathing waveforms are never opened, and nothing is sent until you press Upload.
